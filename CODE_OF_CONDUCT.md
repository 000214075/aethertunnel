# 行为准则 · Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 · Our pledge

为了营造一个开放且受欢迎的环境，无论身份如何，我们都承诺让参与本项目的每个人免受骚扰。
为了营造一个开放且受欢迎的环境，我们承诺让参与本项目及社区的每个人都免受骚扰，无论年龄、
体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、
国籍、个人外貌、种族、宗教，或性倾向与性别认同。

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and
maintainers pledge to make participation in this project and our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 我们的标准 · Our standards

**鼓励的行为**：对其他参与者表示同情；不同意时对事不对人；优雅地接受建设性批评；
聚焦于对整个社区最有利的事情。

**不可接受的行为**：使用性化的语言或意象； troll、侮辱性或贬损性的评论以及人身或政治攻击；
公开或私下骚扰；未经明确许可发布他人的私人信息（如地址或电子邮箱）；其他可被合理认定为
不适合专业环境的行为。

**Positive behaviour**: empathy toward other participants; disagreeing with ideas, not
people; accepting constructive criticism gracefully; focusing on what is best for the
community.

**Unacceptable behaviour**: sexualized language or imagery; trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments and personal or political attacks; public or private harassment;
publishing others' private information without explicit permission; any conduct that can
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## 责任 · Responsibilities

维护者有权利与责任删除、编辑或拒绝与本行为准则不一致的评论、提交、代码、wiki 编辑、
issue 与其他贡献；必要时会说明裁决理由。

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this
Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for such decisions when appropriate.

## 适用范围 · Scope

本准则适用于项目空间内（issue、PR、代码评审与讨论），也适用于代表项目在公共空间的场合。

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces (issues, pull requests, code review
and discussions) and whenever an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## 执法 · Enforcement

违反本准则的行为可透过 GitHub issue 联系项目维护者举报；所有投诉都会被审阅和调查，
并作出必要且适当的回应。维护者若未能真诚遵守或执行本准则，将面临由项目其他领导层
决定的临时或永久后果。

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to
the project maintainers through a GitHub issue; all complaints will be reviewed and
investigated, and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the
circumstances. Maintainers who fail to enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the
project's leadership.
